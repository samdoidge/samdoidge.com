X-COM: Apocalypse came out in 1997 as a DOS game. On a modern Mac — particularly Apple Silicon — it doesn't just run. It needs a DOS emulator, a working config, and a way to launch it without typing commands every time.

I wrote up the whole setup, verified on macOS 26 with DOSBox Staging, and put it in one place: **[xcom-apocalypse-mac](https://github.com/samdoidge/xcom-apocalypse-mac)**.

It covers config for both DOSBox Staging 0.82 and 0.83, a double-clickable `.app` wrapper, saves, and the usual troubleshooting. You still need to own the game — the repo contains no game data.

That's the pointer. The README is the guide.
